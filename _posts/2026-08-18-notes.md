* "*Most organisational systems have settled into a steady state over time and serve their purpose well enough. If the business environment demands a different system behaviour, the system will actively resist by wanting to revert to its previous state. It's like trying to push a car out of a ditch: the car keeps rolling back until you finally get it over the hump. This system effect makes organisational transformation so challenging.*" - Gregor Hohpe
