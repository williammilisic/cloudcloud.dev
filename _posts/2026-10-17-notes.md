* “*War is peace, freedom is slavery, ignorance is strength*” is the official motto of the ruling Party in George Orwell's dystopian novel 1984. 
<br/><br/>
Meaning of the Slogans
<br/>
• **War is peace**: Permanent external conflict unites the population through shared hatred of an enemy, prevents them from focusing on internal oppression, and maintains state stability. 
<br/>
• **Freedom is slavery**: Standing alone as an individual brings isolation, weakness, and failure; true security and safety come from surrendering personal will and serving the collective Party. 
<br/>
• **Ignorance is strength**: Keeping the population uneducated, blind to contradictions, and unable to think critically prevents rebellion and reinforces the absolute authority of Big Brother.
